Lens application third-party notices
=====================================

This file covers the components that ship in the payload beside the
executable itself. The OCR runtime under ocr/ carries its own notice, and the
local voice runtime carries another one beside it.

Qt 6 (6.9.0)
------------
The application links Qt 6 dynamically; the Qt DLLs ship beside the
executable under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 3 (LGPL-3.0).
The complete license text ships beside this notice as
LICENSE-Qt-LGPL-3.0.txt, and the source of the Qt distribution this build
used is available from https://www.qt.io/download/open-source-source.
Because the linkage is dynamic, a reader may relink the application against
a modified Qt of their own and ship the result under the application's own
MIT license.

(Copyright (c) 2008-2009 Bjoern Hoehrmann, MIT). Statically linked from

Microsoft Visual C++ runtime
----------------------------
The payload carries the MSVC runtime DLLs (msvcp140*.dll, vcruntime140*.dll,
concrt140.dll, vccorlib140.dll) taken from the Visual C++ redistributable
that matches the build toolchain, deployed application-local under
Microsoft's redistribution terms (docs/adr/0207). They are Microsoft
copyright, not open source; the VC++ redist license from the toolchain
governs them.
