# What this answers without a pointer: the panel comes up, the learning page routes in, and the page
# still paints after scrolling to the group that carries auto-play, accent, engine, fallback and the
# local voice line. It does not answer whether the speaker glyph is drawn on a word card -- that

#   powershell -File scripts/qa/lens-dev.ps1 -Script scripts/qa/scenarios/pronunciation.txt

surface show settings

surface route settings learning
wait painted:settings

surface scroll settings 700
