# The pronunciation page, a category of its own (docs/adr/0134, UI-SPEC.md 4.4).
#
# What this answers without a pointer: the panel comes up, the pronunciation page routes in, and the
# page still paints after scrolling to the rows that carry auto-play, accent, engine, fallback and the
# local voice line. It does not answer whether the speaker glyph is drawn on a word card -- that
# needs a card, and a card needs an answer; the bubble cases in lens_qtest_surfaces own that.
#
# Run with:
#   powershell -File scripts/qa/lens-dev.ps1 -Script scripts/qa/scenarios/pronunciation.txt
state
surface show settings
wait painted:settings
surface route settings speech
wait painted:settings
# The page scrolls; 700 is far enough that the local voice line is on screen.
surface scroll settings 700
state
quit
