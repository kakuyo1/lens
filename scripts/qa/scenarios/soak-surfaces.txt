# One round of the surfaces the developer face can actually raise.
# Only these seven carry an opener in dev_surface.cpp's kOpeners; the other surfaces in
# surface_table.h are raised by a product flow (selection, review, update, onboarding) and
# cannot be shown by name. A round therefore exercises seven of the windows, every time.
#
# The painted wait matters: the first time a window reaches the screen is when the D3D device,
# its driver thread pool and its handles are paid for. Counting only show/hide would measure
# a property write, not a first frame. See docs/metrics/resource-footprint/glass-memory-2026-10-18.md.
#
# Run one round on its own:
#   powershell -File scripts/qa/lens-dev.ps1 -Script scripts/qa/scenarios/soak-surfaces.txt
# Drive it repeatedly and sample the curve:
#   powershell -File scripts/qa/lens-soak.ps1 -Script scripts/qa/scenarios/soak-surfaces.txt -Rounds 50
state
surface show settings
wait painted:settings
surface hide settings
wait hidden:settings
surface show tray
wait painted:tray
surface hide tray
wait hidden:tray
surface show stats
wait painted:stats
surface hide stats
wait hidden:stats
surface show words
wait painted:words
surface hide words
wait hidden:words
surface show yearWords
wait painted:yearWords
surface hide yearWords
wait hidden:yearWords
surface show cost
wait painted:cost
surface hide cost
wait hidden:cost
surface show profile
wait painted:profile
surface hide profile
wait hidden:profile
surface hide-all
state
quit
