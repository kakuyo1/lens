# 0202 The glass releases its capture when no sheet is live

- **Status**: accepted (2026-11-20; Stage 1 of OPTIMIZATION-PLAN.md, the fix after the attribution round; stays inside the adapter ADR 0121 left to GlassBackdrop)
- **Background**: A single glass surface ignites Windows Graphics Capture on the screen duplication refuses, and the session was opened on the first read and kept for the process lifetime. The attribution round (`docs/metrics/resource-footprint/idle-attribution-2026-11-20.md`, section 5) measured Private Data 87.3 → 371.7 MB on the first show and still 421.5 MB five seconds after hiding the last surface. The refresh timer stops when no sheet is live, but nothing released the session: a measured floor of 490.9 MB private at hide+120 s, against 295.1 MB idle, with the device's 34 threads and 1,204 handles still there.
- **Decision**:
  - `GlassBackdrop` arms a single-shot 10 s timer whenever the refresh plan stops the timer (no live sheet, or the Eco tier, which does no live work). A plan that runs again disarms it. On expiry the backdrop re-checks the plan and queues one release per screen session on its read pool.
  - `GlassCapture::release()` and `GlassDuplication::release()` tear down the session, the frame pool, the staging texture and the D3D11 device, without setting the permanent `closed` flag and without touching the refusal memory; the next read's existing `ensure()` opens a new session. Both call `IDXGIDevice3::Trim()` before dropping the device, a hint to the driver that the process is idle.
  - Each frame pool gets an epoch, bumped under the frame mutex when the pool opens and again when release() tears it down; the handler carries its pool's epoch and drops its frame unless the epoch still matches. A handler already in flight finishes before the clear, and one queued from a closed pool stores nothing. Without that guard a frame could land after the pool was cleared and keep its device alive.
  - The release runs on the one pool thread, so it lands after any read in flight and on the thread that opened the WinRT apartment and the device (ADR 0087's thread-affinity rule). The sheets keep their last picture, so a reopened card still draws a frosted still until the first fresh frame lands. Refresh pacing, the sheet's live/fingerprint behaviour and the visuals are untouched.
- **Consequences**:
  - The release line is written 10.0 s after the last sheet hides. Measured on the release tree: threads 218 → 184, handles 6,207 → 5,002, Private Data at hide+120 s 244.7 → 113.5 MB in the lightest run (the AMD capture threads and `GraphicsCapture.dll`'s worker are gone; 32 `nvwgf2umx` threads remain, most likely the shared device's glass-rendering pool (ADR 0191), since the capture device is on the AMD adapter). Private floor: 335.4 / 346.9 / 434.5 / 357.4 / 333.8 MB in five clean runs against 490.9 MB before, with idle 290.4–294.6 MB.
  - The remainder is the drivers' per-process high-water inside Private Data, not the session: in the clean runs Heap is flat at 158.9–161.7 MB and the final threads and handles are the same, while the floors differ by 100 MB. So D1's "idle +50 MB within 60 s" is met in the two lightest runs (+42.6 and +42.0 MB) and missed in the other three (+55.5, +144.8, +62.8 MB) and in the one with a mid-run VMMap (+129.0 MB). The spread's cause was left open here. The next round withdrew the frame-traffic explanation (seven runs, Pearson −0.22; three runs of 668–675 frames spread +42.6 to +144.8 MB) and took the shared-device trim, which lowered the floor to +12.5 to +16.9 MB (ADR 0203, `docs/metrics/resource-footprint/glass-idle-residual-2026-11-20.md`).
  - Reopening pays a new ignition: 151–176 ms from `surface show` to the capture log line on this machine, while the card painted from the held picture in 99–140 ms. A card closed and reopened inside the 10 s window stays warm and pays nothing; that warm window itself was not measured this round (no close-and-reopen within the grace period).
  - The last desktop picture stays in memory while no sheet is live, as it did before: never written to disk, never logged (ADR 0034, 0035). The frames the session itself held are dropped with it.
  - `Glass*` unit suites 58/58 green; no test expectation changed.
- **Alternatives rejected**:
  - **A session for the process lifetime** (the status quo): exactly the floor this round removes; the timer already stops, so nothing read the screen while it paid.
  - **Sharing the capture device with the app's shared D3D11 device now**: a larger change across ADR 0191 and ADR 0087 (the scanout adapter and the cross-GPU copy), and it does not decide when a session ends. Kept as the next lever for the driver high-water.
  - **Releasing from the GUI thread, or on `liveChanged()` directly**: the frame pool and device belong to the reading thread's apartment, and an immediate release would make every quick close-and-reopen pay the ignition again.
  - **Dropping the sheets' pictures too**: a card would reopen empty, and the pictures are a few MB, not the cost this decision targets.
