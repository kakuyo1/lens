Two Cranes at the Harbour

Mara Ellison · October 2026

By morning the harbour was busy. Above the quay, a crane swung a steel beam over the water while, further out, a pair of birds lifted from the reeds and turned for the marsh.

Nobody on the quay looked up. The crane kept its slow rhythm, and the birds kept theirs, until the light changed and the whole harbour went quiet at once.
