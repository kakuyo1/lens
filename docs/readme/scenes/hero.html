The Art of Noticing

Mara Ellison October 2026 Reading

Serendipity, she wrote, is not luck. It is attention. She had come to the archive looking for one thing and found another: a letter that changed the shape of her book.

The distinction matters. Luck arrives; attention makes room for it. A reader who stops to ask what a word is really doing is already reading more closely than one who turns the page.

“Attention is a form of hospitality,” she wrote. “It makes a place for the unexpected.”From the notebook

That is why the old reading rooms still feel different from a feed. Nothing there is trying to move you along. The page waits while a sentence opens, and the word you did not know becomes part of the room.
