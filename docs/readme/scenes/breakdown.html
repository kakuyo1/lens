The Art of Noticing

Mara Ellison · October 2026

Serendipity, she wrote, is not luck. It is attention. She had come to the archive looking for one thing and found another: a letter that changed the shape of her book.

The distinction matters. Luck arrives; attention makes room for it. A reader who stops to ask what a word is really doing is already reading more closely than one who turns the page.

By morning the square was empty. Although the rain had stopped, the streets remained flooded for hours. Nobody who lived here said why.
