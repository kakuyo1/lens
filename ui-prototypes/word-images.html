Essay · The river

Low Water

Mara Ellison · October 2026

The tide had gone out further than I had ever seen it. Barges sat on their own reflections; the reeds ticked in the wind.

The crane was the only thing moving on the river that morning.

I watched it for a long time. Then the light changed, and the whole scene went from grey to gold.

Perhaps that is what I had come for: not the bird or the machine, but the pause between the two.

“A word is a way of looking. Sometimes you have to look twice.”
